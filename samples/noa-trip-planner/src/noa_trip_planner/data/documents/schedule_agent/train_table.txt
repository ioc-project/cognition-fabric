TRAIN TYPES
================================================================================

EXPRESS TRAINS:
- Fewer stops, faster service
- Premium seating available
- Complimentary WiFi and refreshments

STANDARD TRAINS:
- More stops, scenic routes
- Standard seating
- Cafe car available

NIGHT TRAINS:
- Sleeping compartments available
- Arrives early morning at destination
