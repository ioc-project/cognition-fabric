TICKET TYPES & UPGRADES
================================================================================

STANDARD TRAINS:

NIGHT TRAINS:
- Base fare as listed above

================================================================================
DISCOUNTS & PASSES
================================================================================

MULTI-TRIP DISCOUNTS:
- Round-trip tickets: 15% discount on total fare
- Weekly pass: $250 (unlimited travel on all routes)
- Monthly pass: $850 (unlimited travel on all routes)

AGE-BASED DISCOUNTS:
- Children under 12: 50% discount on base fare
- Seniors (65+): 30% discount on base fare

Note: Discounts cannot be combined. Upgrades are charged at full price.
